If income or credit falls short

Private landlords (marked on cards) often care more about a steady job and a good first impression than a strict income multiple. Message them first.

A guarantor who earns about 80x the rent can co-sign. Or a guarantor company such as TheGuarantors or Insurent co-signs for a fee, usually around one month's rent. Big complexes usually accept them.

Offer more upfront. An extra month of security deposit can win over a small landlord. In NJ the deposit is capped at 1.5 months' rent.

How places are picked and scored

Pet rules and income requirements are read from each listing's text, so "Pets: ask" means the listing doesn't say. New Craigslist and Zumper/PadMapper apartments under $1,700, refreshed every few hours, within about 40 minutes' drive of Morristown. Newark, Irvington and Elizabeth are left out. The score (0–100) is price 30%, drive to Morristown 30%, the town's violent-crime rate 20% (NJ State Police, 2023, per 1,000 residents; the state average is about 2), cats allowed 10% and easy terms 10% (private landlord, no strict credit or income rule), minus 15 for each warning (5 for "unusually cheap" or "no photos"). The bars on each card show each part. Today's top 5 are the best new places from the last day with no warnings, skipping ones you've already sorted. Utilities are estimated at $150 a month. Warnings are guesses: never send money before seeing a place in person and meeting the landlord.
